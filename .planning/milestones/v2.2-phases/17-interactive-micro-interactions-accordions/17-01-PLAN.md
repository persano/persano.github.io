# Plan 17-01: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Accordions

## Goal
Implement tactile press feedback on buttons and badges, hover lift and shadow elevation on cards, and animated smooth icon rotation and content transition on FAQ accordions.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Active Press Feedback on Buttons & Badges (INT-01)
- In `css/base.css`:
  - Add active press damping (`transform: scale(0.97)`) on `.badge-cta`, `.form-submit`, `.consent-banner button`, `.lang-select`, and links styled as buttons.
  - Set transition on `transform var(--dur-micro) var(--ease-spring-snappy)`.

### Task 2: Hover Lift on Cards (INT-02)
- In `css/base.css`:
  - Within `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`:
    - Add hover lift (`transform: translateY(-2px); box-shadow: var(--elevation-high); border-color: rgba(217, 169, 81, 0.4);`) to `.feature-group`, `.app-card`, `.mode-item`, and `.changelog-entry`.
    - Apply transition: `transform var(--dur-interaction) var(--ease-spring-gentle), box-shadow var(--dur-interaction) var(--ease-spring-gentle), border-color var(--dur-interaction) ease`.

### Task 3: Smooth FAQ Accordion Expansion & Icon Rotation (INT-03)
- In `css/base.css`:
  - Upgrade `.faq-item summary::after` indicator:
    - Instead of swapping text `+` to `\2212`, keep a single `+` glyph and animate `transform: translateY(-52%) rotate(45deg)` when `.faq-item[open] summary::after`.
    - Transition: `transform var(--dur-interaction) var(--ease-spring-snappy), color var(--dur-micro) ease`.
  - Add smooth transition on `.faq-item` summary and content.
