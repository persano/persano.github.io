GeoHist Trivia

Pack your bags — we're traveling through time.

A fast, friendly quiz game where history meets geography: guess countries from their flags, place cities on the map, and date the events that shaped the world. How far back can you go?

What's packed inside

Trivia

  • Multiple game modes based on History and Geography — flags, capitals, maps, events and more.
  • An interactive UI built entirely with Jetpack Compose for a modern, fluid experience.
  • Integrated background music with a perceptual volume curve and interactive sound effects.

Google Play Games

  • Seamless sign-in, cloud saves, achievements, and leaderboards.

Offline

  • The core game requires no internet connection.
  • Achievements are queued and synced once a connection is established.

In-App Purchases

  • Unlock premium content or support development — try the Remove Ads option.
