Frequently asked questions

Does GeoHist Trivia collect my data?

The App works mostly locally (offline-first) and does not collect or store personal identifying information on its own servers. All game progress, levels, medals, and settings are saved locally on your device. The App and this website use third-party services provided by Google: AdMob (Google Mobile Ads), Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Firebase Analytics (website only), and Firestore via the contact form (website only). Messages are deleted after they are handled.

Can I play offline?

Yes — the App works mostly locally (offline-first), so you can play wherever your travels take you. All game progress, levels, medals, and settings are saved locally on your device.

What devices does it run on?

GeoHist Trivia runs on Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer — no flagship phone required.

Are there in-app purchases?

Yes — optional purchases let you unlock premium content or support development. Google Play Billing handles secure in-app purchase processing (for example, the Remove Ads option). Payment data is handled entirely by Google; we never access your financial data or credit cards.

What languages is the game available in?

English, Spanish, Portuguese and 17 more languages.

Where is my progress saved?

Right where you left it. All game progress, levels, medals, and settings are saved locally on your device.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Send your request through the contact form using the "Request data deletion" topic, or write to santiagopostorivo@gmail.com. Every request is handled manually: the messages are deleted and you get a confirmation by email. Messages are deleted after they are handled.

What is the game about?

A fast, friendly trivia adventure across history and geography — flags, capitals, maps, famous figures, and the events that shaped the world. Think of it as a museum you can argue with.

Who makes GeoHist Trivia?

GeoHist Trivia is published by Santiago David Postorivo — a one-person studio with a soft spot for old maps and older stories.
