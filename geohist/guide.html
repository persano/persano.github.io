How to play

GeoHist Trivia is quick to pick up and hard to put down — here's the two-minute version before your first expedition.

  1. Pick a category and a game mode — flags, capitals, maps, events, art, or coats of arms.
  2. Answer the timed questions — read the hint, trust your gut, and tap your answer.
  3. Build streaks — chain correct answers to keep your run alive and the score climbing.
  4. Earn medals and climb the leaderboards — every expedition turns your travels into trophies.

Game modes

Eight ways to wander the world — each one a different lens on history and geography.

  • Flags

    See a flag, name the country — no packing required.

  • Capitals

    Match each capital to its country — quickfire geography for the globally minded.

  • Map figures

    Pin famous figures where history put them — read the map, take your guess.

  • Cities & islands

    Find cities and islands on the living map — from megacities to specks in the ocean.

  • Events in time

    Place historical events in their year — was it ancient history or just yesterday?

  • Art detective

    Recognize legendary figures in art — painted portraits, sculpted heroes, and more.

  • Coats of arms

    Match heraldry to its homeland — a little blazon goes a long way.

  • World Passport

    The grand tour — journey through every collection and fill your passport with the whole world.

That's the whole crash course. The full feature list, the FAQ, and your ticket to Google Play are waiting on the game page.
