What's new in GeoHist Trivia

Every app update, newest first — new expeditions to take, changes to how the game plays, and fixes along the way.

Entries below are shown in English.

0.99998 — 2026-10-27

Fixed

  • The crashes reported from the previous build are fixed.
  • Purchase and billing state now behaves correctly, including offline retries.

Changed

  • Every language tile in the picker shares the same height, including Urdu.
  • A deep reliability pass: voice narration, offline play, multiplayer with a second device and the rest of the phone checklist are tested and verified.
  • Leaner release builds after repo cleanup.

0.98 — 2026-10-10

Fixed

  • Map clues and the Encyclopedia show real rendered maps again, never a blank placeholder.
  • Live duels are more reliable — matches hold together and results land where they should.

Added

  • A full desktop version — Windows, macOS and Linux get all 21 screens, sounds, artwork and every language.

Changed

  • Weekly leagues play out more realistically.
  • The iOS build was audited end to end and repaired where the audit found damage.

0.92 — 2026-10-05

Added

  • Live duels — challenge other players in real time.
  • A gold economy: earn gold and spend it on what you want.

Changed

  • A new navigation bar with icons and labels, so every screen is one tap away.

0.91 — 2026-09-23

Added

  • Four historical scholar companions who guide you through the game.
  • A new procedural soundtrack and sound effects.

Changed

  • Difficulty now flows with your performance instead of jumping between fixed levels.
  • The whole interface was rebuilt on one consistent design system — unified spacing, buttons and cards.
  • Every screen now holds up on small phones and in all 20 languages.
  • Automated screenshot tests guard the interface so nothing drifts.

0.88 — 2026-09-06

Added

  • The Question Factory — write your own trivia questions and put them in front of other players.
  • Five historical Conquest eras to play through, each with its own map and borders.
  • A Proficiency Radar showing where you're strong and where to study next.

0.86 — 2026-09-02

Added

  • The Conquest campaign in chronological order — play history from ancient times onward.
  • Weekly bot leagues to climb when no one else is online.
  • Habit reminders: optional push alarms that keep your streak alive.
  • Optional in-app purchases.

0.84 — 2026-09-01

Added

  • Pin-Drop mode — answer by dropping a pin straight on the map.
  • The Study Hub: a Leitner-box reviewer that brings facts back right before you'd forget them.
  • Custom decks — build your own question sets and play them.
  • 1080×1080 share cards for posting your results.

0.7 — 2026-08-27

Added

  • HD flag graphics across the game.
  • Daily quests — a fresh challenge waiting every day.
  • Achievement badges for milestones big and small.
  • Progress analytics graphs so you can see how you're improving.

0.5 — 2026-08-25

Added

  • Landmark and Battle question categories.
  • A 7-day streak calendar to track your run.
  • Google Play Games cloud save — your progress syncs across devices.
  • Pass & Play — two players share one device and take turns.

Changed

  • Clearer map polygon styling for map-based clues.
  • Core performance and stability polish across the game.

0.1 — 2026-08-21

Added

  • Offline basemaps — map questions work without a connection.

Fixed

  • A round of crash and stability fixes from the first playable build.

Changed

  • Questions moved into a local database — faster, more reliable rounds.
  • Interface integrity fixes across the main screens.
  • Accents and diacritics normalized across questions, so names read correctly in every language.
